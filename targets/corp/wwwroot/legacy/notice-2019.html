Notice: Sheffield foundry renews quality certification

Posted 5 August 2019

Meridian Castings is pleased to confirm that the Sheffield foundry has successfully renewed its ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 certification, following a site-wide process audit carried out over the previous two weeks.

The audit covered melting, moulding, finishing and final inspection processes, and included a review of supplier approval records and customer complaint handling. No major non-conformances were raised.
